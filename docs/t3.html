RS^N³·T³ · Hopf: three closed axes, no fibre

The Hopf page puts a closed fibre w on top of an open cube. Here the three axes x, y, z are themselves closed, so the quantum that the fibre added is already in each axis, and the page has the same determinants without a fourth axis. At maximal current, det Σ / |det Ω| = (C°(dx) C°(dy) C°(dz))² (product states, six observables). The open cube equals 1 exactly when every axis has 2 or 3 positions (3×3×3 saturates); on T³ a ring of 3 already has C°² = 5/4, so no box saturates. Each ring tends to 2, the box to 8, and no finite box reaches it.

What is not here

A fourth axis w is not part of T³. The Hopf reading of w as the fibre of physical space is a declared bridge (a premise) in the repository, not a theorem, and this page does not use it. In Lean the closed axis d = 3 keeps its band and proper time (ClosedThreeBand), the ring limit is RingSzego, and the cube inside the box is untouched by what w carries (CubeInBox).
